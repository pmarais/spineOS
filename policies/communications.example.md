# Policy: outside communications

**Owner:** business-manager · **Applies to:** every member and every agent · **Review:** quarterly

1. Every outbound message to a client or outside party is a GATED action (SPINE.md §4): staged for
   review, sent only on a recorded, single-use authorisation.
2. One send per authorisation. A send that looks failed is verified against the channel record
   before anyone concludes anything. Never re-send on the old authorisation.
3. We never offer what policy does not allow. If a sentence lets a reasonable reader reply
   "yes please", it is an offer, and an offer is a commitment.
4. Client messages use controlled language: one idea per sentence, 25 words maximum, no idioms.
   Most readers skim; one fact misread costs more than ten sentences saved.
5. Read the WHOLE inbound message before acting on any part of it. Requests ride on payment
   confirmations and get lost while the money gets processed.
6. Before recording any wait ("blocked on them"), check the inbound channel. A quiet log means WE
   went quiet.
